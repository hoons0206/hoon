인간은 언제 미술적인가?


When is a person in an ‘artistic’ state? An ‘artistic’ state refers to the experience of visual information being transformed into a sense of existence through a formal composition.


An ‘artistic’ state arises in the fleeting moment when a formal composition is interpreted as a trace of existence, regardless of whether one is in a gallery or standing in front of a dressing table.


At such times, the role of the creator is to significantly increase the likelihood of this ‘artistic’ state occurring.
